Led by someone who navigated this system — and decided to change it.

Theodore L. Barnes, M.S.Ed. built Empowered Education from a conviction formed across nearly two decades in higher education — as an enrollment advisor, campus builder, admissions director, faculty member, and research scientist.

Assistant Professor of Career Development

Research-grounded. Impact-oriented.

Every program and product is informed by active scholarship. Our founder is a published co-investigator in empirical research on AI and academic integrity — and his doctoral work at Loyola University Chicago is shaping how we understand the relationship between AI and student intellectual agency.
