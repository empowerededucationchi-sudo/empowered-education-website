Consulting services

AI is already in your classrooms. The question is whether your institution is ready.

Empowered Education helps colleges, universities, and school districts navigate artificial intelligence with equity, clarity, and confidence — through faculty training, policy design, curriculum integration, and institutional strategy grounded in real classroom experience.

Built for educators and administrators, not just technologists

Faculty development, academic integrity policy, AI curriculum integration.

K–12 districts

Workforce programs

AI readiness training for adult learners, community colleges, and job training orgs.

Nonprofits & CBOs

AI ethics orientation, staff training, and community education programming.

AI Literacy Faculty Training

Half-day or full-day workshop for faculty and instructional staff

$3,500

half-day / up to 25

A structured workshop that moves faculty from anxiety to agency around AI. Covers what AI tools actually do, how students are already using them, and how to design assignments that engage AI thoughtfully. Grounded in equity — addresses how AI tools perform differently across demographic groups.

  • Pre-workshop faculty survey and needs assessment
  • Facilitated workshop (up to 25 participants)
  • AI literacy slide deck licensed for internal reuse
  • Discipline-specific assignment redesign examples
  • Post-workshop resource packet + 30-day follow-up support

Why us

Credibility built in classrooms, not just conferences

Active faculty, not outside observers

We teach undergraduate students and build curriculum in D2L. We know what faculty actually face — not from a distance, but from the same rooms.

Research-grounded practice

Our AI literacy framework is informed by doctoral research in equity and agency in higher education, and by published empirical work on AI and academic integrity (Wan et al., 2025).

Equity is not a disclaimer

Every engagement explicitly addresses how AI tools interact with race, class, and access — because those questions don't disappear if you don't name them.

Nonprofit mission alignment
