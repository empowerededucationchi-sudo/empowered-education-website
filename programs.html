The Bridge Mentoring Program

High school seniors + college student mentors + career professionals

Bridge pairs high school seniors with trained college student mentors and career professionals for a structured semester-long experience. Participants build genuine professional relationships, learn how higher education actually works, and enter college with a network — not just a backpack.

What participants leave with:

  • A trained college student mentor in a related field of interest
  • At least one job shadow experience with a career professional
  • Completed professional profile (LinkedIn, resume, email etiquette)
  • Understanding of financial aid, campus resources, and office hours culture
  • Language for discussing social capital and how to build it intentionally

The Capital Workshop

College students training to teach social capital in high schools

The Capital Workshop is a two-day intensive that trains college students to facilitate social capital conversations with high school seniors. Participants become credible near-peer educators — young people who have recently navigated the transition themselves.

Topics covered: professional identity, networking, navigating institutional culture, college prep timelines, and how to talk about money, class, and career without shame.

Who we serve

There is a role for everyone

High school student

Apply to join Bridge as a mentee. Open to juniors and seniors in the Chicago region. No GPA requirement — just commitment.

College student

Mentor a high schooler through Bridge, or apply for Capital Workshop training to become a near-peer facilitator.

Career professional

Offer a job shadow. Two hours can permanently shift a student's sense of what is possible for them.

School or college

Partner to bring Bridge or Capital Workshop to your students. We work with counselors, advisors, and student affairs offices.

FAQ

Frequently asked

Do I need a certain GPA or test score to apply?

No. Our programs are open to any student who is curious, committed, and willing to show up. Academic performance is not a selection criterion.

Is there a cost to participate?

All Empowered Education programs are free to students. Access to mentorship and professional networks should never depend on a family's ability to pay.

Do you serve students outside Chicago?

We currently operate primarily in the Chicago region but are building toward national and international reach. Virtual participation options are available for select programs.

How do schools or organizations partner with you?

We welcome partnerships with high schools, colleges, community organizations, and employers. Reach out to start a conversation.

Ready to join or partner?
